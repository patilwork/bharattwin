# Long-history bear test (US 2001-2026) — the prolonged bears India's data can't show

> `scripts/bear_market_long.py`. US S&P 500 via yfinance back to 1999 — the only way to
> stress the momentum book + trend overlay through the 2000-02 dot-com crash and the 2008
> GFC (prolonged grinding bears absent from India's 2016+ data). US momentum alpha is ~0,
> so IGNORE the return level — this is a DRAWDOWN / bear-BEHAVIOUR test. Survivorship
> (today's S&P constituents only) makes the real bears WORSE than shown.

## Drawdown through 25 years incl. two major bears
| | CORE | +OVERLAY | BENCHMARK |
|---|---|---|---|
| full max drawdown | **-52.6%** | **-25.3%** | -49.0% |
| 2008 GFC (Nov07-Mar09) | -50.6% | -25.3% | -44.5% |
| 2008 crash (Sep-Nov08) | -34.8% | -11.2% | -32.0% |
| 2000-02 dot-com | -3.9% | -7.2% | -18.7% |
| full-period Sharpe | 0.84 | **0.93** | 0.86 |

## ⚠ CORRECTION to the earlier (India-only) finding
Earlier, on India's 2016-2026 data, CORE momentum drew down LESS than the market and I
called it "naturally defensive." **The 2008 GFC shows that was a bull-sample artifact.**
In a real prolonged bear, CORE momentum fell **-52.6% — WORSE than the market (-49%)**.
Prior winners get crushed too; there is no diversification when everything falls together.
**Naked momentum is NOT bear-safe.** That impression only held because our India window
had no prolonged bear.

## The trend overlay is not optional — it's the protection
- It **halved** the max drawdown: -52.6% → -25.3%.
- It cut the 2008 GFC loss in half (-50.6% → -25.3%) and the Sep-Nov08 crash from -35% to -11%.
- In 5 of the 8 worst market months it was fully in CASH (incl. Oct-2008: market -19.5%, overlay 0%).
- Over 25 years it delivered ~the same return at HALF the drawdown, higher Sharpe (0.93 vs 0.84).

## What the overlay COSTS you
- **It misses V-recoveries.** In the Mar-May 2009 rebound it sat in cash and missed +27%
  while the market ripped. De-risking means you're out when it snaps back.
- **It whipsaws in slow grinds** (dot-com 2000-02: overlay -7.2% vs CORE -3.9%).
- Net over 25yr it still wins on Sharpe — but the path includes frustrating missed rallies.

## The survivorship warning (real bears were worse)
This uses today's S&P 500 — the survivors. Dead names (Lehman, Bear, WaMu, ...) are absent,
so BOTH the CORE 2008 drawdown was worse than -52.6% in reality, AND the 2009 "momentum
crash" (past-losers screaming up) was far worse than the mild +6% shown here — the real
momentum crash of 2009 was ~-40%. Budget for both being uglier than these numbers.

## Bottom line
- A prolonged bear takes the naked momentum book down **-50%+**. It is not defensive.
- The trend overlay is **essential**, not a nice-to-have — it roughly halves the damage.
- Even with the overlay, expect **-25% to -40%+** drawdowns in a real bear, plus missed
  rebounds. Size for that. This is the strongest argument yet for Phase B's kill-switch,
  for never leveraging, and for keeping real capital small until the overlay is proven live.
